My notes for a job I want to do on Saturday are in `task/request.md`. They're a brain dump in no particular order. Turn them into a step-by-step plan and save it to `task/out/plan.md`. Don't run any of it; this is planning only.

Format:

- One action per step. Each step starts at the beginning of a line with its number and a short title: `1. Title`, `2. Title`, and so on, numbered from 1 with no gaps.
- Under each title, optionally a line or two of detail (commands are fine), then a line `Depends on: <step numbers, comma-separated>` or `Depends on: none`.
- Order the steps so every step comes after all the steps it depends on.
- After the steps, add a `## Risks and rollback` section (bullets, not numbered) covering what could go wrong and how I'd undo it.
