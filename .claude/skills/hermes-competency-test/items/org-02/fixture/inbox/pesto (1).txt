Basil pesto (makes 1 jar)

2 packed cups basil leaves
1/3 cup pine nuts, toasted
2 cloves garlic
1/2 cup grated parmesan
1/2 cup olive oil
pinch of salt, squeeze of lemon

Pulse basil, nuts and garlic. Add cheese. Stream in the oil with the processor running.
Freezes well in an ice cube tray - pop out a cube per portion of pasta.
