# Chili - what worked

- 500 g beef mince + 1 chorizo, browned hard
- 2 onions, 3 garlic, 2 tbsp chipotle in adobo
- 1 tin black beans, 1 tin kidney beans, 2 tins tomatoes
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 square dark chocolate at the end
- simmer 2 hours, better the next day

Next time: less salt, the chorizo is salty already.
