Banana bread

3 very ripe bananas, mashed
1/3 cup melted butter
1/2 cup brown sugar
1 egg
1 tsp vanilla
1 tsp baking soda, pinch of salt
1 1/2 cups flour
handful of walnuts

Oven 165 C (fan). Mix wet, then dry. Loaf tin, 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
Less sugar than the first version and nobody noticed.
